# Access, Entry & Parking — Zen Japanese Massage Platinum

Master copy for the six Platinum locations. Written to be pasted straight into
booking confirmation emails, website location pages, and Google Business profiles.

**Locations covered:** Bondi Junction · Surry Hills · St Leonards · West End · Teneriffe · Auckland CBD

**Structure:** Part 1 is what every guest needs regardless of branch. Part 2 is the
one-line summary of all six. Part 3 is the full detail per branch. Part 4 is the short
form for confirmation emails. Part 5 lists what still needs checking before publishing.

---

## Part 1 — Before you arrive (all locations)

> Use this block once, at the top of a location page or confirmation email. It is true
> everywhere, so it never needs to be repeated per branch.

### 📍 Finding us

- Five of our six studios sit **inside a Salon Lane complex** — Bondi Junction, Surry
  Hills, St Leonards, West End and Teneriffe. Look for the Salon Lane entrance, not a
  Zen shopfront.
- **Auckland CBD is the exception**: we are on level **B1** of a residential building,
  reached by an external stairway. See the Auckland section for the exact approach.

### 🚪 Getting in

- **If the door is locked, use the intercom**: type **"Zen"** and select **"Zen Japanese
  Massage."**
- A closed door does not mean we are shut — it usually means therapists are in session.
  Please wait a moment at the door rather than leaving.

### ✅ Signing in

- On entry, **scan the QR code at Reception** to sign in for your appointment.

### ☕ Customer Lounge

- Once signed in, relax in our Customer Lounge. **Complimentary coffee and tea** are
  available to enjoy both before and after your massage.

### ⏰ Timing

- Arriving **5 minutes early** gives you time to sign in and settle without cutting into
  your treatment time.
- Where parking is time-limited or needs validation, the parking note for your branch
  says so — read it before you set off.

## Part 5 — To confirm before publishing

Everything above comes from the existing location copy plus public listings. These
points were not verifiable from that material and should be checked by the branch:

1. **Walking times.** No minute counts are quoted anywhere in this document, because
   none could be verified. Add them per branch if you want them ("3 min walk from
   Bondi Junction Station").
2. **Public transport lines.** Nearest stations and stops are named, but exact routes,
   stop names and the Auckland rail situation should be confirmed locally.
3. **Suite / shop numbers.** Bondi Junction, Surry Hills, St Leonards and Teneriffe are
   listed by street address only. If Salon Lane uses a suite or shop number at any of
   them, add it — it is the single most common cause of a guest arriving late.
4. **Parking rates and free periods.** Free-parking allowances change. Re-check the
   Westfield app allowance, the West Village 2-hour period, and the 77 Nicolson Street
   validation arrangement before this copy goes live.
5. **Phone numbers.** Deliberately omitted. Add a per-branch contact line if guests are
   expected to call when they cannot get in.
6. **Sign-in and lounge.** The QR sign-in and complimentary coffee/tea are stated as
   applying to all six. Confirm that Auckland CBD runs both the same way — the existing
   Auckland copy does not mention them.
7. **Scope.** Enmore and Wollongong are intentionally excluded from this document, which
   covers the six Platinum branches only.
