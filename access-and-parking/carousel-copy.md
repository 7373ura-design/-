## Page 2 — Before You Arrive

- **Title:** Before You Arrive
- **Intro:** The same five minutes at the door, wherever you're booked. Here's what to expect before your therapist calls you through.

1. **Look for Salon Lane** — Five of the six studios are inside a Salon Lane complex, not a Zen shopfront.
2. **Locked door? Use the intercom** — Type "Zen" and select "Zen Japanese Massage."
3. **A closed door isn't a closed studio** — It usually means therapists are in session. Wait a moment at the door.
4. **Scan to sign in** — The QR code at Reception checks you in for your appointment.
5. **Customer Lounge** — Complimentary coffee and tea, before and after your massage.
6. **Arrive five minutes early** — Enough time to settle without cutting into your treatment.
