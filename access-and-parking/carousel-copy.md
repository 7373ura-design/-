## Notes on the build

- **Transport lines were cut from the branch pages.** The body text boxes hold about 200
  characters before overflowing the cream card, so each page carries entry + parking only.
  Nearest stations are in `access-parking-guide.md` if you want them on the page — they
  would need a longer text box or a smaller size.
- **Two photos repeat.** Six studio pages draw on four backgrounds from the source
  carousel, so Bondi Junction / Auckland CBD share one photo and Surry Hills / Teneriffe
  share another. Swapping in per-branch photography would fix this.
- **The St Leonards row on page 10 is the highlighted one**, inheriting the "Most popular"
  emphasis style — it is the only studio that needs the guest to do something (ask for
  validation), so the emphasis carries meaning.

## Open items

Walking times, suite numbers and current free-parking allowances are unverified — see
`access-parking-guide.md`, Part 5. Page 10's note is worded so the carousel stays accurate
even if an operator changes their free period.
