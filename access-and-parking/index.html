Part one

Before you arrive — true at every studio

Say this once, at the top of a location page or confirmation email. Nothing here needs repeating branch by branch.

Look for Salon Lane

Five of the six studios sit inside a Salon Lane complex. Look for the Salon Lane entrance — there is no separate Zen shopfront.

Locked door? Use the intercom

Type “Zen” and select “Zen Japanese Massage.” A closed door usually means therapists are in session, not that we are shut.

Scan to sign in

On entry, scan the QR code at Reception to sign in for your appointment.

Customer Lounge

Relax in the lounge once you have signed in — complimentary coffee and tea, before and after your massage.

Arrive five minutes early

Enough time to sign in and settle without cutting into your treatment.

Check your parking note first

Free periods and validation differ by branch. Read the branch note before you set off, not on arrival.

One exception — Auckland CBD. It is not a Salon Lane site: the studio is on level B1, reached by the stairway beside the Barclay Apartments entrance. If the door is closed, therapists are in session — wait by the door.

Bondi Junction

34 Bronte Road, Bondi Junction NSW 2022 · inside Salon Lane
Entry

Part five

To confirm before publishing

Drawn from the existing location copy and public listings. These points could not be verified from that material.

  • Walking times. No minute counts are quoted anywhere, because none could be verified. Add them per branch if you want them — “3 min walk from Bondi Junction Station”.
  • Transport lines. Nearest stations and stops are named, but exact routes, stop names and the Auckland rail situation should be confirmed locally.
  • Suite and shop numbers. Bondi Junction, Surry Hills, St Leonards and Teneriffe are listed by street address only. If Salon Lane uses a suite number at any of them, add it — it is the most common reason a guest arrives late.
  • Parking rates and free periods. Re-check the Westfield app allowance, the West Village two hours, and the 77 Nicolson Street validation before this goes live.
  • Phone numbers. Deliberately omitted. Add a per-branch contact line if guests are expected to call when they cannot get in.
  • Sign-in and lounge at Auckland. The QR sign-in and complimentary coffee/tea are stated as applying to all six. The existing Auckland copy mentions neither — confirm it runs the same way.
  • Scope. Enmore and Wollongong are intentionally excluded; this covers the six Platinum branches only.
